Mixture Correlation Analysis
============================

This analysis compares the observed expression in mixture samples with an "in silico" mixture profile 
derived from the pure component samples (e.g., Brain and Kidney).

The expected profile is calculated as the weighted mean of the pure components' expression. 
Correlation (Spearman) is then computed between each mixture sample and this expected profile.

The boxplot shows the distribution of correlation coefficients across replicates for each mixture condition.
