SQANTI3 Structural Categorization
==================================

Distribution of reconstructed transcript models across SQANTI3 structural categories (FSM: Full-Splice Match, ISM: Incomplete-Splice Match, NIC: Novel In Catalog, NNC: Novel Not in Catalog, and Antisense/Intergenic) for experiment {{ snakemake.wildcards.experiment }} using tool {{ snakemake.wildcards.tool }}.

Provides structural quality control and novelty categorization for long-read transcriptome discovery.
